MoodTraker Privacy Policy

MoodTraker is a mood tracker: one square per day, a whole year on one screen.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Españolbaltajmn@gmail.com

The short version. MoodTraker has no accounts and no server of ours. Your moods, your days, your notes, your tags and your photos are files on your phone. We cannot read them, we have no copy of them, and we could not hand them to anyone if we wanted to. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into MoodTraker: the mood you pick for each day, the notes you write, the tags you add, the names and colours of your moods, your reminder time, and any photo you attach to a mood. All of it lives in the app's private storage, which no other app on your phone can read.

There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, your email address or your phone number, because there is nothing to attach them to.

Photos

When you attach a photo to a mood, Android's or iOS's own picker hands the app that one image, and the app copies it into its private storage so it can draw it in the year grid and on share cards. The copy never leaves your device. MoodTraker never reads your photo library: it only ever receives the single image you chose in the system picker, and it does not ask for gallery permission.

Backups you make

Export writes your moods, days and photos into one file and hands it to the system share sheet, so you decide where it goes. Once you have saved it, that file is yours and is outside our control. If you put it in a cloud folder, that provider's policy applies to it, not this one.

Backups Android makes

If Android's backup is switched on, your phone may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That copy belongs to you and to Google, not to us: we never see it and cannot reach it. You can turn it off in Android's backup settings.

What leaves your device

Today: nothing

As shipped, MoodTraker makes no network requests at all. It has no server to talk to.

When purchases are switched on

MoodTraker offers a single one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed by the store you installed it from, Google Play or the App Store. We never see or receive your card details, your billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether a given installation has bought Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history attached to it, and technical device identifiers. Because MoodTraker has no accounts, that identifier is not linked to your name, your email address or any profile. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Nothing else

MoodTraker contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone. The only reason the app would ever reach the network is to check your purchase status.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the day or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the app store are kept for as long as those services need in order to honour restores and to meet their own legal and tax obligations.

Children

MoodTraker is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app has no accounts, no public content and no social features.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

In practice you can exercise most of these yourself, instantly, because the data is on your phone: deleting a day erases it, uninstalling erases everything, and Export is data portability. For the purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to baltajmn@gmail.com and we will act on your request. Note that we can only find your records if you give us the anonymous identifier or the store order number, precisely because we hold nothing that identifies you.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (delivering the purchase you made) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent restores. You also have the right to complain to your supervisory authority. In Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Your rights under the CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident: in the last twelve months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (an anonymous installation identifier and device identifiers) and "commercial information" (your purchase history), for the sole purpose of delivering the product you bought.

We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not knowingly sell the personal information of anyone under 16. You have the right to know, to delete, to correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Write to baltajmn@gmail.com.

Do Not Track

MoodTraker does not track you across apps or websites, so there is no tracking behaviour to change in response to a Do Not Track signal.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Changes that affect what leaves your device will also be described in the app's release notes.

Política de privacidad de MoodTraker

MoodTraker es un registro de ánimo: un cuadrado por día, el año entero en una pantalla.

Última actualización: 17 de septiembre de 2026

Englishbaltajmn@gmail.com

La versión corta. MoodTraker no tiene cuentas ni servidor nuestro. Tus ánimos, tus días, tus notas, tus etiquetas y tus fotos son ficheros de tu teléfono. No podemos leerlos, no tenemos copia y no podríamos dárselos a nadie aunque quisiéramos. Desinstalar la app los borra.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Todo lo que pones en MoodTraker: el ánimo de cada día, las notas que escribes, las etiquetas que añades, los nombres y colores de tus ánimos, la hora del recordatorio y cualquier foto que pongas en un ánimo. Todo vive en el almacenamiento privado de la app, que ninguna otra app de tu teléfono puede leer.

No hay registro. Nunca te pedimos tu nombre, tu correo ni tu teléfono, porque no hay nada a lo que asociarlos.

Fotos

Cuando pones una foto en un ánimo, el selector del propio sistema le entrega a la app esa única imagen, y la app la copia a su almacenamiento privado para poder dibujarla en la cuadrícula del año y en las tarjetas para compartir. La copia no sale del dispositivo. MoodTraker no lee tu galería: solo recibe la imagen que tú eliges en el selector, y no pide permiso de galería.

Las copias que haces tú

Exportar crea un fichero con tus ánimos, tus días y tus fotos, y te lo entrega a la hoja de compartir del sistema para que decidas dónde va. Una vez guardado, ese fichero es tuyo y está fuera de nuestro control. Si lo dejas en una carpeta en la nube, se le aplica la política de ese proveedor, no esta.

La copia que hace Android

Si tienes activada la copia de seguridad de Android, tu teléfono puede guardar una copia de los datos de la app en tu propia cuenta de Google y devolvértela al reinstalar o al cambiar de teléfono. Esa copia es tuya y de Google, no nuestra: nosotros no la vemos ni podemos acceder a ella. Se desactiva en los ajustes de copia de seguridad de Android.

Lo que sale de tu dispositivo

Hoy: nada

Tal y como se publica, MoodTraker no hace ninguna conexión de red. No tiene ningún servidor con el que hablar.

Cuando se activen las compras

MoodTraker ofrece un único pago dentro de la app, por una sola vez. El pago lo procesa la tienda desde la que la instalaste, Google Play o la App Store. Nosotros no vemos ni recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta, tu dirección de facturación ni ningún otro dato de pago.

Para saber si una instalación ha comprado Pro, la app usa a RevenueCat, Inc. como encargado del tratamiento. RevenueCat asigna a cada instalación un identificador anónimo y guarda, por cuenta nuestra, ese identificador, el historial de compra asociado e identificadores técnicos del dispositivo. Como MoodTraker no tiene cuentas, ese identificador no está ligado a tu nombre, a tu correo ni a ningún perfil. Su política de privacidad.

RevenueCat trata los datos en Estados Unidos. Las transferencias fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo están cubiertas por las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea.

Nada más

MoodTraker no lleva SDK de publicidad, ni de analítica, ni de informes de fallos. No hacemos perfiles, no hacemos remarketing y no vendemos ni compartimos información personal con nadie. La única razón por la que la app tocaría la red es comprobar el estado de tu compra.
